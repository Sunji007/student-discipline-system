# Quiet Continuity

Cream space and deep green typography form a continuous visual surface. Carefully balanced margins preserve the original composition and make each page feel part of the same family.

Institutional identity appears as a compact pair of authentic emblems. Their proportions remain intact, with painstaking alignment and consistent optical scale across every page.

Hierarchy stays clear: identity occupies the upper corner, followed by the main subject and its supporting evidence. Meticulous spacing keeps the emblems distinct from the title without competing with the content.

Refinement comes from replacing existing elements rather than adding decoration. Patient attention to consistency, restrained color, and precise placement creates a coherent academic presentation.

Replaced the upper-left text with the Yala Rajabhat University and IT department logos on all 18 pages of the existing editable Canva presentation. Source logos: docs/manual_images/logo_university.png and logo_department.png. The remaining slide content and layout were preserved.
